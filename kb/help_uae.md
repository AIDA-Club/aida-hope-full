# Where to get help in the UAE (all emirates)

**Chatbot rule:** share only the CONFIRMED details below. **Never share anything in the "Not yet confirmed" section.** If the person's emirate is not covered, or you are unsure, say "please check the official website or ask a doctor," and give the emergency numbers if it is urgent. Do not invent phone numbers. All details were checked on 10 Oct 2026.

**Be honest about limits:** the UAE has no single national booking line for breast screening that we could confirm. Screening is arranged through the person's own health authority, health centre or insurer. Where we have an official number, it is listed. Where we do not, send the person to the official website or their doctor.

## Screening advice used by UAE authorities — CONFIRMED
- **Emirates Health Services (EHS)** (ehs.gov.ae, early detection of breast cancer page): monthly breast self-examination from age 20; a mammogram "every two years starting from age 40"; women under 40 with a family history should get annual screening.
- **Abu Dhabi Public Health Centre (ADPHC)** (adphc.gov.ae): monthly self-exam from age 20; a clinical breast exam every 3 years at ages 20–39; a mammogram every 2 years at ages 40–75 (the page also mentions an annual clinical exam from 40); women at higher risk may be advised to be screened more often.
- Rules for who gets free or insured screening differ by emirate and provider. Always say "ask your health centre or insurer."

## Dubai — CONFIRMED
- Dubai Health Authority (DHA) news, 10 Dec 2017 ("Basmah" initiative): the basic benefit plan of the Dubai mandatory health insurance scheme covers breast cancer screening and treatment, following international screening criteria. If cancer is found, the DHA Patient Tracking and Follow-Up Centre is notified to help with treatment.
- This was an official announcement from 2017, so say "as announced by DHA" and advise checking with the person's insurer or clinic.
- **Al Jalila Foundation** (Dubai Healthcare City; a charity that funds treatment and research, not a screening clinic): phone +971 800 255 25452 and info@aljalilafoundation.ae, as printed on its Contact Us page. Address: First Floor, Building 12, Dubai Healthcare City.
- No current DHA phone line could be confirmed. DHA's own site blocked our checks.

- Pink Caravan page: https://focp.ae/pink-caravan-campaign (modified 10 Sep 2025). It says Pink Caravan has brought "free screenings, mammograms, and hope to communities across the UAE since 2011." It lists no 2026 clinics, so for current locations call FOCP.
- Patient support programmes (focp.ae Moral Support Programs page): Color My World (psychological wellbeing); Locks of Hope (wigs after treatment); Joy Cart (children with cancer); Birthday For A Cause (reducing isolation).

## Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah — CONFIRMED
- Public healthcare in the northern emirates is run by **Emirates Health Services (EHS)**: https://www.ehs.gov.ae
- EHS's "Initiate Action, Receive Hope" campaign (ehs.gov.ae news, 3 Sep 2024) offered free screening to women aged 40 and above using 20 mobile clinics with mammography and ultrasound. It started in Ras Al Khaimah, expanded in 2024 to Ajman and Umm Al Quwain, and ended on 26 Nov 2024. **It is a past campaign. Do not say it is running now.** No booking number was given.
- EHS breast awareness page: https://www.ehs.gov.ae/en/awareness/noncommunicable-diseases/early-detection-of-breast-cancer (it notes that radiology at Al Madam Health Centre was temporarily suspended; so always call a centre first).
- No EHS phone number could be confirmed (see conflict below), so tell people to visit their nearest EHS health centre or the website.
